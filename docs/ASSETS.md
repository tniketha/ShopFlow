# Logo and product photos

Author and logo owner: **tniketha**.

The logo combines a shopping bag and an S-shaped arrow. The app uses forest green (`#163D31`), lime (`#D8E6A3`) and a warm background (`#F6F5EF`).

Files in `frontend/public/brand`:

- `shopflow-mark.svg`: mark for light backgrounds.
- `shopflow-mark-light.svg`: mark for dark backgrounds.
- `favicon-32.png`: browser icon.
- `apple-touch-icon.png`: mobile bookmark icon.
- `shopflow-preview.png`: repository/portfolio preview.

## Product photos

Inventory, checkout and stock alerts use bundled reference photographs matched to recognized product names. Unknown product types display a neutral package icon. Reference photos illustrate the type of product, rather than an exact brand or package. All images are local assets and require no external image requests.
